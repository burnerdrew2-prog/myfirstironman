Skip list · where protein money dies

Don't buy

  1. Jerky and meat sticks — ~6–9g protein per dollar. A fifth of chicken thighs.
  2. Protein bars and RTD shakes — you own a 5 lb bag of whey; a bar is whey with a 4× markup and candy glaze.
  3. Pre-cut fruit, snack packs, cart drinks at the course — convenience taxes, all of them.
  4. A second chicken tray before 20 August — the freezer has to be empty for the move.
Costco is the freezer. H-E-B is anything that spoils.

The standing lists

Costco · every 2–4 weeks

  • Chicken breast / tenderloin — 6.5 lb tray (×2 only after the move)
  • Lean ground beef 96/4 — 4-pack of 1.33 lb tubes
  • Top sirloin — 4–5 lb, cut into steaks and freeze separately
  • Frozen sockeye fillets — individually wrapped
  • Large eggs, 24-ct ×2 — you eat 28 a week
  • Whey isolate, 5 lb · 2% cottage cheese ×2 · nonfat Greek yogurt 48 oz
  • Jasmine rice 25 lb (after the move), rolled oats 10 lb
  • Pitted Medjool dates, 2 lb — four dates is 25g carbs, your bike fuel
  • Electrolyte drink mix, 50 packets — the most important item here
  • Frozen berries, frozen broccoli, olive oil, raw almonds, canned albacore, honey

H-E-B · weekly

  • Chicken thighs family pack — the 47g/$ champion, roast with the batch
  • Baby spinach 16oz, bell peppers, onions, garlic, limes, romas, cucumber, cilantro
  • Mootopia milk — 12g protein a cup instead of 8
  • Black beans ×4, ground turkey 2 lb, deli turkey, block cheddar
  • Rotisserie chicken — one at a time, it keeps 4 days
  • Avocados — two or three at different ripeness, never a six-pack
  • Mi Tienda corn tortillas, bakery sourdough, bagels for pre-ride
  • Russets 5 lb, bananas ×2 bunches, oranges, apples, Gatorade powder, salsa

Spoilage & the 20 minutes that prevent it

  • Portion the day you buy. 6.5 lb of chicken becomes thirteen 8oz bags before it touches the fridge.
  • Fresh chicken & ground beef — 2 days. Freeze on day zero.
  • Rotisserie — 4 days · cottage cheese — 6 · spinach — 5 · milk — 7
  • Sourdough — 4 days, or slice and freeze half
  • Freezer check: a month of Costco meat is ~30 lb. After 20 Aug: go every two weeks, buy half.
The weekly batch · 90 minutes, one oven, two burners

The cooking

Sheet-pan chicken + broccoli

4 lunches · 25 min, mostly oven
  • 425°F. Pat 2.5 lb breasts (or thighs) dry, oil, salt, smoked paprika, garlic powder.
  • 20–22 min to 160°F internal, rest 5 under foil. Don't eyeball it.
  • Last 15 min: frozen broccoli on a second tray, oil + salt.
  • Slice, box with rice. Four ~10 oz portions.

Taco beef

5 servings · 20 min, one pan
  • Widest pan, high heat. Brown 2.66 lb 96/4 in two batches — crowd it and it steams grey.
  • One diced onion in with batch two; a can of black beans, drained, at the end.
  • Last 2 min: 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1½ tsp salt, splash of water.
  • Tacos and rice bowls through the week.

The rice pot

~11 cups cooked · hands-off
  • 4 cups jasmine, rinsed. 6 cups water, big pinch of salt.
  • Boil → lid on, low, 12 min → off heat 10 min. No peeking.
  • Cool flat, one container. 60-second microwave portions — the carb targets live here.

While the oven runs

10 min of parallel work
  • Hard-boil 8 eggs: rolling boil, 10 min, ice water.
  • Overnight oats ×3 jars: 80g oats, 1 scoop whey, 240ml milk, honey.
  • Bag dates in fours — ride and golf pockets.
