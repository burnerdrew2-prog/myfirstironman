The week · both phases

What a week is

Twelve-ish hours. Four swims because frequency is what builds a stroke, two rides because the bike is a volume problem, two or three runs because the run is already in you, two lifts because more than that steals recovery from the sport that has a gate on it.

The eating clock · timing, not just totals

When to eat

Daily targets live on the Diet page and on every date of the Daily view. Timing is the other half: the same calories placed badly train worse and recover slower. This is the consensus position of the sports-nutrition literature, sized to you at ~73 kg.

MomentWhatThe reasoning
1–4 h before a hard session1–4 g/kg carbs + 20–30g protein. Practically: rice + chicken 3h out, or oats + whey 90 min out.Tops off glycogen; protein before ≈ protein after for muscle protein synthesis.
15–60 min before morning swimsBanana + 20g whey + coffee. Light — not a full stomach.You train fasted-ish otherwise; a small feed protects the session without sloshing.
During, under 75 minWater only.Fuelling short sessions just adds calories you don't need.
During, 75 min +60g carbs/hr — bottle of sports drink + a gel or four dates.30–60g/hr is the evidence-backed range for >60–70 min efforts; you're at the top of it because the bike is your growth sport.
During, rides 2 hr +70–90g carbs/hr, 500–750ml fluid/hr, 500–700mg sodium/hr.August in Texas is a salt problem, not just a water one. Practise race-gut from week 5.
Within 45 min after anything long40g protein + 60–80g carbs. Shake + banana counts.Glycogen re-synthesis is fastest in the first 2 h; it matters because your next session is always <24 h away.
Every 3–4 hA real feed: 35–45g protein each, 4–5 a day.165g/day split evenly beats the same grams in two meals for muscle retention.
Pre-bedCottage cheese or milk-based feed.Slow casein protein overnight; also the easiest feed to hit when the day ran long.
Race morning600–800 kcal, low fibre, 3 h before the 06:40 start. Then 80g/hr on the bike, 40–60g/hr on the run.Rehearsed on every long ride from week 5 — nothing new on race day.

Rules that outrank motivation

Hard lines

  1. 8 hours of sleep is a training session. The two short nights the plan tolerates (late Fridays) are scheduled, not accidents.
  2. The swim gate is dates, not vibes: 800 yd by 30 Aug, 1,500 yd by 6 Sep, open water 13 Sep. Miss the first → buy coaching that week.
  3. No cutting before 5 October. Maintenance carbs are what make the build absorbable. The six-pack is a December outcome.
  4. Nothing new on race day — fuel, kit, pacing all rehearsed from week 5.
