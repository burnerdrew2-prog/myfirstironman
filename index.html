    The swim gate

    The bike is a volume problem and the run is already in you. The swim is a skill, it happens in open water with nothing to stand on, and it does not respond to effort on a schedule. So it gets dates instead of opinions.

    Sun 30 Aug
    800 yd
    Continuous, pool, no wall stops.
    Sun 6 Sep
    1,500 yd
    Continuous. The real checkpoint.
    Sun 13 Sep
    Open water
    1,500+ yd in a lake, in race kit, never alone.

    Miss 30 August and you buy coaching that week without relitigating it. Miss 6 September badly and you defer to Galveston, 4 April 2027 — registration runs to 24 March, so the fallback costs nothing. That is exactly why the checkpoint has to be one you honour rather than argue with.

    The build

    Six training days, one lighter day. Four swims a week, because frequency is what builds a stroke. Weeks after the gate are drawn faint — real, but provisional on 6 September.

    Spoilage & the 20 minutes that prevent it

    • Portion the day you buy. 6.5 lb of chicken becomes thirteen 8oz bags before it touches the fridge. Skip this and you bin three pounds in ten days.
    • Fresh chicken & ground beef — 2 days. Freeze on day zero.
    • Rotisserie chicken — 4 days · cottage cheese — 6 · spinach — 5 · milk — 7
    • Sourdough — 4 days, or slice and freeze half
    • Almonds live in the freezer past two months
    • Freezer check: a month of Costco meat is ~30 lb. Sharing a kitchen after 20 Aug means going every two weeks and buying half.
