What thirteen months of Hevy actually said

Why this program

Your own log, 2,276 rows: 102 sessions in 13 months — 1.8 a week, drifting to one a week since March. Twenty lower-body sessions total, none since 11 May, no barbell squat or deadlift anywhere. Lateral raises appeared 52 times and kickbacks 48 — accessories crowding out compounds. The research you did points the same place the data does: compounds first, close to failure, hit everything at least twice a week, stop. So this is two 45-minute days that fit inside a triathlon build — the program the data says you'll actually do, not the one that looks best on paper.

Progression · the boring rules that work

How it moves

  1. Add 5 lb when every set completes clean at effort ~2 reps shy of failure. Miss a week? Repeat the weight. No maxing during the build.
  2. Squat earns load with depth first. 135 until 3×5 hits depth on video; then normal progression.
  3. The accessories you love are the dessert, not the meal. If laterals and kickbacks return, they go after the compounds, never instead.
  4. During race week (28 Sep–4 Oct): one light full-body session Tuesday, nothing after Wednesday.
  5. Cut phase, from 5 Oct: same lifts, keep the load, drop one set each, protein up to 180g — muscle keeps mass it's asked to keep.
  6. Log everything in Hevy, same as the 102 before it. The next program gets designed off this data.
